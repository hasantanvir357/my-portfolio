ABOUT ME

EXPLORE NOW

Clean architecture is the foundation of everything I build. I craft responsive, polished interfaces with React & Next.js — component-driven development, careful state management, and pixel-perfect styling with Tailwind CSS. On the backend I design REST APIs with Node.js & Express, model data in MongoDB, and wire up secure authentication flows so the whole system scales. I focus on quality engineering: readable code, accessible interfaces, and a seamless experience across every device.

With a strong grasp of the full MERN stack, I treat every project as a chance to build something better than before — from the smallest utility to a full production application. I believe great software is invisible: it just works, it feels effortless, and it never gets in the way of the person using it.
